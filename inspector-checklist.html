Inspector Business Protection Checklist

Five minutes. Answer honestly. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

1. Income gap

Could you cover your bills for 90 days if you couldn’t work?

Do you have disability coverage?

Does your family know what you earn and owe?

2. Keeping the business running

Could another inspector finish your scheduled jobs?

Are your client list, report templates, and software logins written down where your spouse or partner can find them?

Do you know what happens to your E&O insurance if you stop working?

3. Equipment and debts

Would business loans, leases, or credit cards stay with you, not your family, if you passed away?

4. Retirement

Do you save something every month outside of Social Security?

Do you have a 401(k), SEP-IRA, or similar plan?

5. Life insurance

Do you have life insurance today?

Is it enough to cover your debts plus a few years of income?

Have you reviewed it in the last two years?
